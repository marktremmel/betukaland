# Digital citizen heroes (class 4.A) and the NEM OKÉ pair: ChatGPT image prompts

## How to use these

Work in two steps per hero, in one ChatGPT chat per hero, so the character stays the same.

1. Paste the STYLE block, then the hero's DESIGN prompt. You get one clean picture of the character. If it drifts from the drawing, say what to fix ("smaller crown", "the stripes are pink, not red") rather than starting over.
2. When the design is right, paste the SHEET prompt in the same chat. That gives the animation strip the game needs.

Rules that save the most time:

- One character per image, plain white background, no text, no ground, no shadow. The game removes the white and cuts the frames.
- Ask for exactly the frame count given (6 for walking, 4 for flying), in one row, all frames the same size and the same height.
- Side view facing right for the sheet, even if the design picture faces the viewer.
- No words inside the picture. Slogans and signs ("Stay safe!", "Don't be mean") go into the game as speech bubbles, so ask for a blank sign.
- Keep each child's own details (colours, stripes, crown, mask) even when they look odd; that is what makes it theirs.
- Save each final sheet as PNG named after the hero (for example `lime_walk.png`, `block_snake_walk.png`) and put them in one folder, `4a_heroes/`, in the betukaland folder. The drawings and the child's name go in the credits.

## STYLE block (paste first, every time)

> Pixel art game character in a cute 16-bit style, like a friendly 1990s console platformer. Clean dark outline, flat colours with one shade of shading, readable at small size (about 40 pixels tall in the game). Big friendly eyes, a kind expression. Plain solid white background. No text, no logo, no ground, no shadow.

## SHEET prompt (paste after the design is right)

> Now make a sprite sheet of exactly this character: a 6-frame side-view walk cycle facing right, all 6 frames in one horizontal row, evenly spaced, the same size and height in every frame, plain solid white background, no text, no shadows.

For flyers use: "a 4-frame side-view flying cycle facing right (wings up, middle, down, middle)". For the snakes: "a 6-frame side-view slither cycle facing right". For the round blob heroes: "a 6-frame side-view bouncy hop cycle facing right (squash and stretch)".

## The NEM OKÉ pair (Süni and Szarka)

These two belong to the Forest School story, so they should look like they come from the same world as each other and as the 4.A heroes.

- **Süni (Hedgy), design:** A small shy hedgehog girl, soft warm-brown spines, cream face and belly, round pink cheeks, big dark eyes, a little pink scarf, holding a short yellow pencil. Shy but warm smile.
  Sheet: 6-frame walk, pencil in hand.
- **Szarka (Magpie), design:** A cheeky young magpie, black and white feathers with a blue-green shine on the wings and the long tail, bright eyes, a confident but not nasty expression (she changes in the story, so she must not look evil).
  Sheet: 6-frame side-view hop-walk on the ground (magpies hop), wings folded.
